# Plot Guidelines: A&A and MNRAS

This file summarizes the core, journal-specified figure/plot constraints for **Astronomy & Astrophysics (A&A)** and **Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society (MNRAS)**. It focuses on column/page widths, expected resolution (DPI/PPI), and font sizing guidance where explicitly stated by the journals.

## Astronomy & Astrophysics (A&A)
[AA guide](https://www.aanda.org/65-author-iformation/information-files)

TL;DR:
 - least 250/300 dpi and not 92 dpi
 - Column - 88 mm, page - 180 mm. Intermediate widths with a side caption are also possible (max. 120 mm
 - No fontsize specified.




## Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society (MNRAS)
[MNRAS guide](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/pages/general_instructions)

TL;DR:
- `Axis labelling, lettering and any plotting symbols should be sized appropriately for the figure and its likely final size of around font size 8 point.`
- Column - 84 mm, no page width specified
- 400 dpi or greater



## Nature 
[Nature guide](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/final-submission)

[Figures specifically](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/preparing-figures-our-specifications/)

TL;DR:
 - preferably 12-point Times New Roman.
 - 89 mm wide (single column) and 183 mm wide (double column), The full depth of a Nature page is 247 mm. Figures can also be a column-and-a-half where necessary (120–136 mm).
 - min 300 dpi, max 450 dpi
 - .pdf or .eps preferred

## Annual Reviews
[guide](https://www.annualreviews.org/upload/static-pages-content/editorial-policies/AR-Graphics-Guide-fullcolor.pdf)

TL;DR:
 - vector, editable, 
 - ` We prepare all figures to fit our layout; you do not need to size
your figures exactly. Ideally, make figures larger and higher
resolution than they need to be; it is easier for us to scale down
as needed than to scale up. The largest image dimensions we can
publish per page are 6.33 × 7.9 inches (160 × 200 mm).`
